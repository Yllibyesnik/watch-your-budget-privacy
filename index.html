Watch Your Budget
Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

In short: everything you put into Watch Your Budget stays on your iPhone. The app does not collect, send, sell or share any of your information.

What the app stores

To work as a budget app, Watch Your Budget keeps the following only on your device, inside the app's private storage:

  • Transactions you enter (date, category, amount, payee and description)
  • Receipt and warranty photos you choose to attach
  • Your budgets, categories, regular payments and warranties
  • Your settings, such as appearance and how the app is unlocked

This information never leaves your phone unless you choose to move it yourself (see "Backups" below).

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in, and no servers: the app does not send your data anywhere.
  • No advertising, analytics or tracking of any kind.
  • No selling or sharing of information with anyone.
  • The developer cannot see your data. Each person who installs the app has their own separate, private budget on their own phone.

Face ID and PIN

If you choose Face ID, the check is done entirely by your iPhone. The app is only told whether it succeeded; it never receives or stores your face data. If you choose a PIN, it is kept in the iPhone's Keychain, Apple's encrypted storage for passwords, and is not sent anywhere.

Photos

When you attach a photo, you pick it using Apple's private photo picker. The app only receives the photo you select, without its location or other hidden details, and it cannot see the rest of your photo library.

Notifications

Payment reminders are created on your phone and shown by your phone. They do not use any internet service.

Backups

When you tap "Back up my data", the app creates a backup file and opens the standard iPhone share sheet. You decide where the file goes, such as the Files app, iCloud Drive, email or AirDrop. Once saved there, it is handled by that service under its own privacy terms. Restoring reads a backup file that you choose.

Links you save

If you save a web link on a warranty and tap it, it opens in your web browser. What happens on that website is covered by its own privacy policy.

Deleting your data

You can delete any entry within the app at any time. Deleting the app from your iPhone removes all of its data from the phone. Any backup files you saved elsewhere stay where you put them until you delete them.

Children

The app is not aimed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
