1. Getting started

  1. Secure the app. The first time you open it, choose Use Face ID or Use a PIN (4 digits). You'll need it every time you open the app.
  2. Allow notifications when asked, so you're reminded the day before a regular payment is due.
  3. You're on the main menu. It has Journal, Dashboard, Reports, Budgets, Regular, Warranties, Categories and Settings, plus How This App Works at the bottom.
Ready-made categories The app starts with common UK categories such as Salary/Pension, Mortgage/Rent, Energy, Council Tax and Groceries. You can add or remove them any time in Categories.
Coming from the older Budget Tracker app? In Budget Tracker, go to Settings → Export my data and save the file to Files. Then in Watch Your Budget go to Settings → Import from Budget Tracker and pick that file. It brings across transactions with photos, categories, budgets, regular payments and warranties.

2. Adding a transaction

  1. Open Journal and tap the green + button.
  2. Check the date (tap it to change).
  3. Choose a category. Expense, Revenue (money in) and Payment (transfers) are all in the list.
  4. Type the amount. The +/− next to it is for the occasional negative amount, such as a refund.
  5. Add the payee (who you paid, e.g. "Tesco") and, if you like, a description (e.g. "Weekly shop").
  6. Optionally tap Add Photo to attach a picture of the receipt.
  7. Tap Save.
Budget warnings If a new entry takes a category to 80% of its budget, or over it, the app tells you when you save.

3. Journal

Every transaction, grouped by month, with this month opened automatically. The figure beside each month is that month's net (money in minus money out).

  • Edit: tap an entry.
  • Delete: swipe an entry to the left. You'll be asked to confirm first.
  • Search: tap 🔍 and type a payee, description, category or amount.
  • Receipt photos: tap the small picture to see it full size. Pinch to zoom.
  • Find duplicates: tap ••• (top right) → Find duplicates. It finds entries with the same day, category, amount and payee, keeps the copy with a photo or description, and lets you delete the extras one group at a time or all at once. Undo appears straight after.

4. Budgets

Set a spending limit for a category, either monthly or annual. Tap +, choose the category, enter the amount and choose the period.

  • Each budget shows a bar for this month (monthly budgets) and this year.
  • The bar is green while you're fine, orange from 80%, and red when you're over.
  • Underneath it says how much is left, or how much you're over.
  • Tap a budget's name to change it, or swipe it left to delete it.

5. Regular payments

Bills and subscriptions that come round regularly: weekly, every four weeks or monthly.

  • The list shows how many days until each one is next due, soonest first.
  • You'll get a reminder at 9am the day before each payment.
  • Weekends and bank holidays: switch these on for payments that go out on the working day before (for example, a payment due on a Sunday goes on the Friday). England & Wales bank holidays are worked out automatically for every year.
  • For every four weeks, enter the date of one known payment and the app counts on from there.

6. Dashboard

This year at a glance: total money in, money out and net, then each month so far with the latest at the top. Tap the clock icon (top right) to look back at previous years.

7. Reports

Open Reports and choose one. Most can be printed or shared as a PDF.

  • This Month / Last Month: every category, with the transactions behind it.
  • Your Own Dates: any start and end date, or a tax year.
  • By Category: one category, for this month or this year.
  • One Payee: everything paid to one name, for this year, last year or all years.
  • Year to Date: money in, money out and net for each month.
  • Year by Category: every category, month by month, on one page.
  • Budget vs Actual: each budget against what you've spent.
  • This Month Breakdown: spending by category. Tap one to see its transactions.
  • Trends: one category, month by month, compared with last year or with its budget.

8. Warranties

Keep track of things you've bought that came with a warranty: the item, purchase date, an optional expiry date, notes, and a link to wherever you keep the receipt (for example a file in iCloud Drive). Tap the link to open it.

9. Categories & transfers

Categories come in three kinds:

  • Revenue: money coming in, such as salary or pension.
  • Expense: money going out, such as groceries or energy.
  • Transfer (shown as "Payment - …"): moving your own money around, such as paying off a credit card or putting money into savings. Transfers don't change your net figure, because no money is really coming in or going out.

Tap + to add a category, or swipe one left to delete it.

10. Face ID & PIN

  • Change how the app unlocks in Settings → Security: Face ID, PIN, or Change PIN.
  • The app locks whenever you leave it. When you come back, Face ID checks you automatically.
  • If Face ID doesn't recognise you, Apple's box offers Enter Passcode, so your iPhone passcode always gets you in.
  • In the app switcher, your figures are hidden behind a cover.
Don't forget your PIN For your security, a forgotten PIN can't be reset. You would have to delete and reinstall the app, then restore your latest backup. Face ID avoids this, because your iPhone passcode works as a fallback.

11. Backups

Your budget is stored only on your phone. If the phone is lost or broken, a backup is the only way to get it back, so back up regularly.

Making a backup

  1. Go to Settings → Back up my data.
  2. In the share sheet, choose Save to Files and pick iCloud Drive (best, because it's kept off the phone), or AirDrop it to a computer, or email it to yourself.

Each backup is a single file named with the date and time, for example WatchYourBudget-backup-2026-11-25-1315.sqlite. It includes everything, photos too.

Reminder If it's been 30 days since your last backup, a "Time to back up" card appears on the main menu. Later hides it for a week.

Restoring a backup

  1. Go to Settings → Restore from a backup and choose the backup file.
  2. The app shows what's in it. Check it, then tap Restore.

Restoring replaces everything in the app with the backup. A copy of what you had is kept, so Undo last restore or import in Settings puts it back if you change your mind.

12. Moving to a new phone

  1. On the old phone: Settings → Back up my data → save to iCloud Drive.
  2. On the new phone: install Watch Your Budget and set up Face ID or a PIN.
  3. Settings → Restore from a backup → choose that file from iCloud Drive.

13. Questions

Can anyone else see my budget?

No. Everything stays on your phone, and nothing is sent anywhere. Each person who installs the app has their own separate budget. See the privacy policy.

Does it sync between my iPhone and iPad?

No. Each device keeps its own data. You can copy everything across using a backup and restore.

I'm not getting payment reminders.

Check iPhone Settings → Notifications → Watch Your Budget and make sure Allow Notifications is on. Reminders are only for payments marked as active.

I deleted something by mistake.

Find duplicates has Undo straight afterwards. Otherwise, restoring your latest backup brings back everything as it was when you made it (and replaces anything newer).
